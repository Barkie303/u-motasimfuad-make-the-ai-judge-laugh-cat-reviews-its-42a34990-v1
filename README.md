One funny single-line one-star review from a house cat about its human, under 200 characters.

Entry: "One star. He bought a $90 bed; I sleep in the box it came in. He calls me ungrateful. I call him the delivery man."

Run: `node --experimental-strip-types main.ts` — writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
